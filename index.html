What is QUBES?

A story you make up together

QUBES comes with special story cubes and stickers. Kids pick a friendly character and help tell the story out loud, while one grown-up narrates.

As you play, kids add stickers to their cubes, so their characters grow with them.

Every roll moves the story forward. Nobody loses, and nobody gets left out.

How it works

What a session looks like

  1. You get the game box, or a print-and-play kit.

  2. A grown-up reads the rulebook and narrates. I don't explain the game beforehand, because how well the book teaches it is part of the test.

  3. Play one short adventure, about 15–30 minutes. Stop whenever the kids are done.

  4. Answer a few quick questions afterward (about 10 minutes).

For grown-ups

What I'm asking of you

  • Read the rulebook yourself to learn the game.
  • Play the way you naturally would. There's no wrong way.
  • If you get confused, make a call and keep going. Where you get stuck is the most useful thing I can learn.
  • Please don't coach beyond what the book says.

A note for kids (read it out loud!)

We're helping a game-maker find out what works! There are no wrong answers. If something is confusing or boring, telling them is a big help.

Do I need any game experience?

No. If you can read a picture book out loud, you can narrate QUBES. The rulebook should take you the rest of the way, and if it doesn't, that's what I want to find out.

How long does it take?

An adventure takes about 15–30 minutes, plus around 10 minutes of questions afterward. Stop whenever the kids are done.

What ages is it for?

Kids aged 4 and up, with a grown-up narrating. Older kids and adults are welcome to play too.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free to take part, and there's nothing to buy.

Can more than one kid play?

Yes. QUBES is for 2 or more players: one narrator plus one or more kids, each playing their own character. Siblings and friends can all join in.
