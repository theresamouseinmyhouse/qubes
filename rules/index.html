Quick Start

Get playing, fast!

One narrator tells the story, everyone else plays a character.

  1. 1

    Narrator sets the scene

    Say where everyone is and what is happening.

  2. 2

    Players say what they want to do

    “Gus wants to try to calm Bonnie down.”

  3. 3

    Narrator names a symbol

    “Give me a Kindness spin!”

  4. 4

    Spin your spinners

    Any matching symbol is a success!

OR

Match the symbol or spin a Wild to succeed!

No match? No problem!

The Narrator adds a Twist. Something changes, and the story keeps moving.

The Symbols

Each spinner will have at least one of these four symbols.

Focus

Thinking and figuring out

Riddles, maps, finding clues

Effort

Might and movement

Climbing, running, lifting, chasing

Kindness

Feelings and connection

Comforting, making friends, being brave

Wild

Matches any symbol

Counts as whatever is asked for

World Spinner

Set the Odds

Before you play, make one World Spinner. It decides how often things go right.

  1. 1

    Take one blank spinner

  2. 2

    Draw a Wild in two spaces

  3. 3

    Draw one Focus, Effort, & Kindness on the spinner

  4. 4

    Leave one space blank for now

2 Wild spaces + 1 of each symbol = a 50% chance to succeed on every spin.

Character Spinner

Make It Yours

Every character gets their own spinner. It starts blank and you choose how it grows.

  1. 1

    Take one blank spinner

  2. 2

    Pick Focus, Effort, or Kindness

  3. 3

    Draw that symbol in one space

  4. 4

    Leave the other spaces blank for now

You can add a Character Spinner any time that character is part of the spin.

Character Spinner

Watch Them Grow

When your character does something big, the Narrator can let you draw a new symbol.

This is Gus the Bus

Gus is very kind, a little clever, and still has room to grow.

This is Bonnie the Bulldozer

Bonnie tackles problems with pure power, but she can also be curious and thoughtful.

A full spinner isn’t the goal

We are looking for characters who have developed in their own way. A blank space is just another Twist.

For Players

Playing a Character

  1. 1

    Talk like your character

    Instead of "I say hi," try "Hi! I'm Gus!" It's more fun, and it's how you bring them to life.

  2. 2

    There's no wrong answer

    Whatever you try, the story finds a way to use it. That's what spinning is for.

  3. 3

    Ask “What if…?”

    Not sure what your character would do? Just say what you're thinking. The Narrator will help.

  4. 4

    Cheer for a Twist, too

    A miss isn't losing, it's a new part of the story. Some of the best moments come from a Twist.

Your character acts however you want

There's no rule book for who your character is, only what your character can do.

For the Narrator

What’s a Twist?

When a spin doesn't match, the story doesn't stop. The Narrator adds a Twist and something changes, but play keeps moving.

It Gets Harder

Something new stands in the way.

The bolt unscrewed, but now the tide is coming in!

Someone Steps In

Another character gets a chance to help.

Bonnie can only do it with help. What will Gus do?

It Costs Something

It works, but not for free.

Bonnie is moving again but used up her tread patch.

A Twist is never “nothing happens.” Something always moves the story forward.

For the Narrator

Running the Game

You don't need a script. A few habits make every session better.

  1. 1

    Ask, don’t tell.

    “What do you see?” or “What does your character do?” gets kids inventing instead of waiting.

  2. 2

    Say yes when you can

    If an idea is fun and doesn't break the story, let it happen and spin for the parts that are uncertain.

  3. 3

    Keep scenes short

    For young players, a scene that resolves in a few spins holds attention better than one that drags.

  4. 4

    Follow their energy

    If they're excited about something you didn't plan, that's the story now.

There's no wrong way to narrate. The goal is a story everyone's excited to keep telling.

Intro Adventure

The Tight Squeeze

N

READ ALOUD“Sunny Port Blossom is buzzing! Tonight is the big festival on Granite Peak Isle, but the Skytide Bridge is still closed for repairs. Looks like everyone is going to have to ride Finley the Ferry, but Silas the Crane has already put the party supplies on Finley’s deck and there’s not enough room for anything else. What are we going to do!?”

The Problem

Finley can’t carry everyone and everything. If the bridge opened, the cars could drive over!

How It Ends

Everyone reaches Granite Peak. If supplies don’t, the party changes to fit.

The Chime Clock

The third chime signals the turning tide, when Finley must set off. Ring a chime when a scene is solved, on the second Twist in a scene, or when play slows down. Friends can always navigate back to an earlier scene.

Resolving the Story

Ways to Win

Any idea that works is a good idea.

Stack

Thank Silas. He restacks and makes room!

Help

Reopen the bridge. The cars drive over!

Wait

Show Finley a plan. He waits for a second trip.

Say yes to little ideas, like squeezing small things into gaps.

Fix one: good. Fix two: great. Fix all three: perfect!

Secrets to Find Reveal when asked

• Silas: A real thank-you gets him restacking.

• Dash cars: They aren’t bad, they just want to play.

• Finley: He waits once if the plan fits the tide.

The Party: Friends First!

  • Tide turns? Everyone rides. Extra stuff waits.
  • Can't ride? The party moves to them.
  • Short on stuff? Make decorations, be the band!
  • Then everyone draws a new symbol on their spinner.

Print & Play

Make Your Spinners

This edition uses paper spinners instead of cubes and stickers. Each spinner has six spaces, just like a cube has six faces.

You need

The spinner page
A pencil
A paperclip
Crayons or markers

How to spin

  1. 1

    Clip on

    Put the pencil point through the paperclip, right on the center dot.

  2. 2

    Hold the pencil straight

    Keep the point still on the dot.

  3. 3

    Flick the paperclip

    It has to go all the way around at least once.

  4. 4

    Read the space

    The far end of the clip points to your result. On a line? Spin again.

How to draw the symbols

FocusA magnifying glass
EffortA lightning bolt
KindnessA heart
WildA star

Spinning more than one?

Spin each one. If any of them lands on the symbol, or on a Wild, it’s a success.
