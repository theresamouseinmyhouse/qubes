# Rulebook changes for the Print & Play (spinner) edition

The Figma rulebook is the master copy. `rules/index.html` is a print-friendly copy for remote playtesters, with cubes and stickers swapped for paper spinners. All other text matches the Figma pages word for word.

Each line gives the page, the Figma text, and the spinner edition's text.

## Not changed

- The four symbol meanings, the World odds (50%), Twists, the intro adventure and the cast are word for word.
- The rulebook is laid out for Letter and A4 instead of its original page shape, so some line breaks differ from Figma.
